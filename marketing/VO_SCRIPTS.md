# Voiceover scripts — Jyotiṣa marketing films

How to use: record each numbered line as its own short clip (phone voice memo is
fine), name them exactly as shown, and drop them into `audio/`. Then run
`bash remux.sh <video>` and I'll lay them over the music at the timings below.

Delivery: calm, warm, unhurried. Think "a trusted friend", not an ad. Leave a
small breath of silence at the start/end of each clip — the mixer handles spacing.
Each cut's on-screen text matches the line, so you don't have to rush to fit.

All start times are measured from 0:00 of the finished film.
